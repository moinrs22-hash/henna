The Secret to Deep Mahogany Stains

Mantasha's Aftercare Secrets

Follow these 4 essential steps after your mehndi application to guarantee a breathtaking, rich dark burgundy color for your wedding photos.

01

Keep It On (6-8 Hours)

Let the organic henna dry naturally and remain on your skin for at least 6 to 8 hours (or overnight) for the henna lawsone dye to penetrate deeply.

02

Lemon & Sugar Nectar

Once dry, dab our special warm lemon and sugar syrup gently with cotton. This seals the paste onto your skin so it continues releasing rich pigment.

03

Clove Steam (Lavang Dhooni)

Heat 4-5 whole cloves on a tawa (pan) and gently warm your hands over the clove fumes. Warmth triggers the natural oxidation of herbal henna.

04

No Water for 12 Hours

Never wash mehndi with water! Scrape off the dried crust with a butter knife or card, then massage mustard oil, coconut oil, or Vicks VapoRub.
